# Civil rights movement

## Key events

- 1954 Brown v. Board of Education: school segregation is unconstitutional, overturning Plessy
- 1955 Montgomery Bus Boycott: after Rosa Parks's arrest, it lasted 381 days. MLK became the movement's best-known leader.
- 1957 Little Rock Nine: Eisenhower sent federal troops to escort Black students into Central High
- 1960 Greensboro sit-ins, and the founding of SNCC
- 1961 Freedom Rides on interstate buses
- 1963 Birmingham campaign, "Letter from Birmingham Jail", and the March on Washington ("I Have a Dream")
- 1964 Freedom Summer in Mississippi
- 1965 Selma to Montgomery march, "Bloody Sunday"

## Laws

The Civil Rights Act of 1964 banned segregation in public places and discrimination in employment. The Voting Rights Act of 1965 banned literacy tests and put federal oversight on places with a history of discrimination. Black voter registration in Mississippi went from about 7% to about 60% within a few years.

## Different strategies

Nonviolent direct action (MLK, SNCC early on) vs Black Power and self-defense (Malcolm X, later SNCC under Stokely Carmichael). Professor Lindqvist said not to treat them as simple opposites, since they overlapped and pushed each other.
