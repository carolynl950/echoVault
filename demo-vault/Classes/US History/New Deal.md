# New Deal

## The Great Depression

The stock market crash in October 1929 kicked it off but wasn't the only cause: buying stocks on margin, overproduction, uneven wealth, bank failures, and then the Smoot-Hawley tariff killed trade. By 1933 unemployment was about 25%. Hoover believed in voluntary cooperation and was slow to give direct relief, so shantytowns got nicknamed Hoovervilles. The Dust Bowl hit the Plains at the same time.

## FDR's first hundred days

The three Rs: relief, recovery, reform.

- Emergency Banking Act + a "bank holiday" to stop the bank runs. Fireside chats on the radio to calm people down.
- CCC: young men planting trees and building parks
- TVA: dams and electricity for the Tennessee Valley
- AAA: paid farmers to grow less so prices would go up
- FDIC: insures bank deposits (still exists)

## Second New Deal

- WPA: millions of jobs, including writers and artists
- Social Security Act (1935): old-age pensions and unemployment insurance
- Wagner Act: protected the right to join unions and bargain collectively

## Criticism and legacy

From the left (Huey Long's Share Our Wealth) it wasn't enough; from the right it was too much government. FDR's court-packing plan in 1937 backfired. The economy didn't fully recover until WWII spending. Still, it permanently changed what people expect the federal government to do.
