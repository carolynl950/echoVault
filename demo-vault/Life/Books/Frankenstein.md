# Frankenstein, Mary Shelley (1818)

Read for fun after using it as the test file for my CS project. Shelley was 18 when she started writing it, during the famous ghost-story contest at Lake Geneva.

## Structure

A frame story inside a frame story: Walton's letters to his sister → Victor's story → the creature's story in the middle. Each narrator is telling it to someone, so you can never fully trust any one of them.

## The creature isn't the monster

He starts out gentle and curious. He teaches himself to read from Paradise Lost and some other books, and he secretly helps the De Lacey family. He turns violent only after everyone rejects him on sight, including the person who made him. Victor runs away the moment the creature opens its eyes. The question the book keeps asking is who the actual monster is.

## Themes

- Ambition without responsibility: Victor creates life and then refuses to take care of what he made
- Isolation: Victor, Walton, and the creature are all lonely in parallel ways
- Nature as healing: Victor keeps going to the mountains to recover

Favorite moment: the creature asking Victor for a companion, arguing that he's miserable because he's alone, not because he's evil.
