# Monthly budget

Income: about $1,100/month from the campus library job (15 hrs/week) + $200 from my parents.

## The 50/30/20 idea

- 50% needs: rent share, groceries, phone, bus pass
- 30% wants: eating out, concerts, guitar strings
- 20% savings and paying off debt

My rent share is $550, so needs are actually more like 65%. I adjusted it to 65/20/15 for now.

## My spreadsheet

Google Sheet with one tab per month. Column A is the category, column B is the budgeted amount, column C is the actual amount, and column D is the difference. Each cell in column D has the formula `=B2-C2` and turns red when it's negative (conditional formatting). The total at the bottom is `=SUM(D2:D15)`. I copy the tab at the start of each month.

## What I learned after 2 months

- Eating out was my biggest leak: $180 in September, mostly late-night food runs with the floor. Now I plan four dinners a week (see [[Weeknight recipes]]).
- Subscriptions add up. I canceled two streaming services I forgot I had.
- Track everything for the first month without judging, THEN set the limits. You can't budget for spending you don't know about.
