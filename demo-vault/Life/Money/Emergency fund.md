Emergency fund = money set aside ONLY for surprise expenses. Not for concerts, not for spring break.

Goal: $1,000 first (starter goal for students), then eventually 3–6 months of expenses once I have a real job.

Why I'm finally doing it: in September my laptop screen cracked and the repair was $240. I put it on the credit card and it took two months to pay off. If the money had already been sitting there, it would have been no big deal.

Where to keep it: a high-yield savings account, separate from checking so I don't see it and spend it. Mine pays 4.1% APY right now. Don't invest it in stocks, because the market could be down right when you need the money.

How: automatic transfer of $50 on every payday. I'm at $410 as of October.
