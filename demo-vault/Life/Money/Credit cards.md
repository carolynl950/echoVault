# Credit cards

## How interest works

APR = annual percentage rate. Mine is 24.99%. If you pay the FULL statement balance by the due date, you pay zero interest (there's a grace period). If you carry a balance, interest is charged daily on what's left, and it compounds.

Example: carrying $500 at 25% APR while only paying the $25 minimum takes about two years and costs roughly $130 in interest.

## Credit score basics

- Payment history: about 35%. Never miss a payment, set up autopay for at least the minimum.
- Utilization: about 30%. Keep the balance under 30% of the limit (under 10% is even better). My limit is $1,000, so I try to stay under $300.
- Length of history: about 15%, so keep your oldest card open
- New credit and credit mix: the rest

## My rules

1. Autopay the full statement balance
2. Only buy things I'd buy with a debit card anyway
3. Never take a cash advance (higher APR, no grace period, plus a fee)
