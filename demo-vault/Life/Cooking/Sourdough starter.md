# Sourdough starter

Got a scoop of starter from Priya's mom. Its name is Doughlores.

## What it is

Flour + water left out so that wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria move in. The yeast make CO2 (that's what leavens the bread), and the bacteria make lactic and acetic acid (that's the sour taste). It's basically the fermentation from bio class happening in a jar on my counter.

## Feeding schedule

- Throw out all but about 50 g, then add 50 g flour + 50 g water (1:1:1 by weight)
- On the counter: feed every 12–24 hours
- In the fridge: once a week is fine, then give it 2 feedings at room temperature before baking

It's ready to use when it doubles in 4–8 hours and has lots of bubbles. Float test: drop a spoonful in water, and if it floats it's ready.

## Troubleshooting

- Gray liquid on top ("hooch"): it's hungry. Pour it off or stir it in, then feed.
- Smells like nail polish remover: also hungry, feed more often.
- Pink or orange streaks: bad bacteria, throw it out and start over.
- Not rising in a cold apartment: put it in the oven with just the light on.
