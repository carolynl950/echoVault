# Weeknight recipes

Cheap, fast, under 30 minutes, can be made in the dorm kitchen.

## Garlic soy stir fry

- 1 lb chicken thighs or firm tofu, cut into cubes
- 3 cups of whatever vegetables (broccoli, peppers, snap peas)
- Sauce: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp cornstarch, 2 cloves garlic, ginger

Sear the protein in batches in a very hot pan until browned, then take it out. Stir-fry the vegetables for 3–4 minutes so they're still crunchy. Add everything back, pour in the sauce, and stir until it gets glossy. Serve over rice. About $2.50 per serving.

## One-pot pasta

Pasta, a can of crushed tomatoes, 4 cups water, garlic, chili flakes, and a handful of spinach all go in one pot. Boil while stirring for about 12 minutes until the pasta is done and the liquid turns into sauce. The starch from the pasta thickens it, so no draining.

## Sheet pan gnocchi

Shelf-stable gnocchi + cherry tomatoes + chopped onion + olive oil, 425°F for 25 minutes. Gnocchi gets crispy on the outside. Add feta at the end.
