# Power chords and strumming

## Power chords

Only two notes: the root and the fifth (sometimes the octave too). No third, so they're neither major nor minor. That's why they sound good with distortion. Written as "5", like E5 or A5.

Shape: index finger on the root on the low E or A string, ring finger two frets up on the next string. The same shape moves anywhere on the neck. Mute the other strings with the side of your index finger.

Basically every punk and rock song uses these. First song: "Smells Like Teen Spirit" intro (F5, B♭5, A♭5, D♭5).

## Strumming

- Keep your hand moving down-up constantly like a pendulum, and just miss the strings when you don't want to hit them
- The most common beginner pattern: D – D U – U D U
- Accents on beats 2 and 4 give it drive

Count out loud: "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &". Downstrums on the numbers, upstrums on the "&".
