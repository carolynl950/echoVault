# Learning chords

Bought a used acoustic in August for $120. Using the JustinGuitar beginner course.

## First chords

The first ones: E minor, A minor, D, G, C, E, A. Em is the easiest (two fingers).

The C to G change is the hardest for me. Tip: find an "anchor" finger that stays in place, or at least moves the least, between two chords.

## Calluses

Week 2: my fingertips are SO sore. Pressing down on the steel strings, especially on the G and C shapes, leaves deep grooves and it stings by the end of a session. My roommate said it's normal and it goes away once calluses form, usually 2–4 weeks of daily practice. What helped:

- Short sessions (15–20 minutes) a few times a day instead of one long one
- Lighter gauge strings (switched to 11s)
- Pressing only as hard as needed, just behind the fret, not on top of it
- Stop when it actually stings, don't push through blisters

Week 5 update: the tips are hard now, and it doesn't bother me at all anymore.

## One-minute changes

Pick two chords and count how many clean changes I can do in 60 seconds. Write it down every day. Em→C went from 14 to 38 in three weeks.
