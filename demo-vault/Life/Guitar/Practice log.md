# Practice log

**Sept 2:** 20 min. One-minute changes Em→C: 22. Started "Horse with No Name", only two chords, good confidence boost.

**Sept 9:** 30 min. Learned the D – D U – U D U strum. Hands don't coordinate yet, the strumming hand stops whenever the other one changes chords.

**Sept 16:** Metronome at 60 bpm. Changes on the beat are rough. Lesson: slow down until it's clean, then go up 5 bpm at a time.

**Sept 30:** First barre chord (F). Sounds terrible, half the strings are buzzing. Every guide says this takes weeks.

**Oct 7:** Used a looper app to record the chord progression and then practiced the strumming over it. Looping the two hardest bars over and over for 10 minutes worked better than running the whole song.

**Oct 14:** F barre rings out on 4 of 6 strings now. Played "Riptide" all the way through for Priya and Sam.

**Oct 21:** Started learning the minor pentatonic scale (position 1, A minor). Improvised over a backing track for the first time. It sounded like actual music for about 4 seconds.
