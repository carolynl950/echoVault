## Research question

Is there a relationship between how many hours students sleep and their GPA?

## Data

Anonymous Google Form sent to our STAT 200 section and two group chats. 64 responses.

Limitations: this is a convenience sample, not random. It's mostly sophomores and probably over-represents students who are organized enough to answer a form. Self-reported sleep and GPA might be inflated (response bias).

## Results

- Mean sleep: 6.6 hours, SD 1.1
- Correlation between sleep hours and GPA: r = 0.41
- r² = 0.17, so sleep explains about 17% of the variation in GPA

## Interpretation

Moderate positive association. But it's observational, so we can't say sleep CAUSES better grades. Possible confounders: students with lighter course loads or fewer job hours might sleep more AND have higher GPAs. Reverse causation is possible too (students who are struggling stay up late catching up).

To actually test causation you'd need a randomized experiment, which isn't ethical or practical here.
