## What actually works for me

Active recall beats rereading. Close the notes and write out everything I remember, then check what I missed. Rereading feels productive but mostly just makes the material feel familiar.

Spaced repetition: review flashcards on day 1, 3, 7, and 14 instead of cramming them all the night before.

Practice problems > notes for stats. Do the old exams under a timer.

For bio, draw the diagrams from memory (mitochondria, chloroplast, the ETC) until I can do them without looking.

## Sleep rule

No all-nighters. In stats we literally learned that sleep and test scores are correlated in our class survey (even if that doesn't prove causation, it's not worth the risk).
