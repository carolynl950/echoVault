## Sunday routine (about 90 minutes)

1. Cook a big pot of rice (6 cups cooked)
2. Roast two sheet pans of vegetables at 425°F: broccoli, sweet potato, chickpeas
3. Marinate chicken thighs for the week
4. Wash and chop raw stuff for snacks
