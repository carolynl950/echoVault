Prompt: "Did Reconstruction fail?" 1,500 words, due Nov 4.

## Thesis

Reconstruction didn't collapse because of its own flaws. Northern leaders abandoned it once protecting Black citizens became politically inconvenient.

## Body 1: real achievements

- The 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments
- Black men voting and holding office: Hiram Revels and Blanche Bruce in the Senate, over 600 Black state legislators
- Public schools across the South for the first time

## Body 2: violence and the retreat

- The KKK, the White League, the Colfax Massacre (1873)
- Enforcement Acts worked for a while, then enforcement faded
- The Panic of 1873 shifted Northern attention to the economy

## Body 3: the Compromise of 1877 as the decision point

- Troops withdrawn in exchange for the presidency
- What followed: disenfranchisement, Jim Crow, sharecropping

## Counterargument

Some historians emphasize mistakes from the start, like no land redistribution. Response: that was also a choice Congress made, which fits the "abandonment" argument.
